# 7‑Day Plan (Polymarket radar + edge hunting)

Goal: ship **1 high-signal post/day** + **2–3 replies** to relevant accounts.

## Day 1 — Setup + first radar post
- Choose your scope: `Ukraine/Geopolitics` + 1 niche (e.g., Kyiv strikes).
- Post a “what I’m tracking” tweet (pin it).
- Reply to 2 accounts posting about Ukraine / OSINT / prediction markets.

## Day 2 — Term structure / adjacent-day anomaly
- Run the Kyiv Edge Scanner.
- Post 1 anomaly with two links + 2 possible explanations.
- Reply to comments with “what would falsify this”.

## Day 3 — Mini dashboard
- Post a 3-market mini dashboard (top by volume or biggest Δ24h).
- Add one sentence: "the common driver seems to be X".

## Day 4 — Process / educational
- Post your "edge checklist" (template #5).
- Invite others to suggest additional checks.

## Day 5 — Market microstructure
- Post about spread + liquidity: show an example of wide vs tight market.
- Explain why *wide spread ≠ edge*.

## Day 6 — Post-mortem
- Pick a market that moved and do a short post-mortem:
  - what you thought
  - what happened
  - what you’d do differently

## Day 7 — Weekly recap
- 5 bullets: biggest moves, best anomalies, lessons.
- Ask for suggestions on what to track next week.

## Simple daily system (15–25 min)
1. Pull top movers (Δ1h/Δ24h) + volume
2. Identify 1 story + 1 chart/number
3. Draft in 5 minutes, edit in 5 minutes, post
4. Do 2 thoughtful replies
